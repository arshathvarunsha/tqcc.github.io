About TQCC

TQCC (Theoretical, Quantum, and Computational Chemistry Research Talks) is a student-driven seminar series focused on modern theory and computation in chemistry and materials science. Our discussions span a wide range of topics, including polaritons and cavity quantum electrodynamics, light–matter interactions, non-adiabatic dynamics, and excited-state processes. We also highlight emerging directions such as machine learning (ML) and AI for chemistry, data-driven modeling, and advanced computational techniques (e.g., electronic structure methods, molecular dynamics, and open quantum systems).

The main goal of TQCC is to make cutting-edge research understandable and accessible—especially for graduate students from different backgrounds. Speakers are encouraged to start from core physical ideas, define key terms, and build intuition before diving into results. This format helps the audience learn the fundamentals while still engaging with current research challenges.

Beyond learning, TQCC is a space to exchange ideas, get feedback, and connect across subfields. We aim to foster new collaborations by bringing together researchers who work on complementary methods and problems. Whether you are exploring a new topic, looking for technical input, or searching for potential collaborators, TQCC is designed to be a welcoming place for discussion.
