About the Research Talks

The Theoretical, Quantum, and Computational Chemistry Research Talks is a seminar series featuring invited speakers and local researchers working across theoretical chemistry, quantum chemistry, and computational molecular science.

Talks typically include a ~45 minute presentation followed by ~15 minutes of Q&A, with informal discussion afterwards whenever the speaker is available.
